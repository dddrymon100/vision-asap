Vision

God’s Word transforming lives in every language in this generation.

Mission

To accelerate Scripture translation and impact for people without God’s Word through Great Commission Partnerships.

Translation and use go together

ASAP includes languages, and the products translated into those languages. But ASAP is also more than that. Success means all Scripture is translated, accessible, and engaged by all people.

Local leaders who want to disciple their communities, but do not have enough Scripture in their heart language, are like builders without the materials they need. A completed translation, without local leaders prepared to make it accessible, is like a well from which no one draws water.

Neither reality alone fulfills the vision. So ASAP holds two realities together as one end state.

All Scripture for all people

People in every language community have access to the Scripture they need for discipleship. That means a full Bible, or an agreed-upon, sufficient set of Scripture.

Local leaders equipped to lead translation and ensure lasting access

Local leaders and institutions have the authority, skills, systems, and resources to lead translation to completion, ensure its quality, and make the completed Scripture accessible to their communities.

When both are true, Scripture does not simply arrive in a language. It becomes part of the life of the community.

Globally

Seed Company is a global ministry. To help shape the global environment for ASAP, we aim to:

  • Localize quality assuranceSee translation quality assurance responsibility and expertise rooted in each local context.
  • Encourage local agencySee translation strategy, management, and resourcing rooted in each local context.
  • Accelerate AI adoption in Bible translationBe a global catalyst for exploring, implementing, and training in AI for translation across the movement.
  • Promote AI for organizational effectivenessPursue effectiveness through AI integration and automation, and share fruitful practices across the movement.
  • Catalyze engagement and impactIdentify fruitful practices for translation that promote Scripture access and engagement by the local Christian community, for lasting impact.

In each country and region

Needs differ from place to place, and so do the Bible translation assets already there. So we work with national and local partners to create a plan for each country or region. The aim is an environment where leaders and institutions can:

  • All ScriptureDefine all the Scripture needed in the languages where, if all people had access to it, they would fully engage.
  • PaceDeliver translation milestones predictably, to complete the first Scripture translation by the planned date while meeting agreed quality standards.
  • Quality assuranceSustain trusted translation quality that fits the context, with clear review methods and community feedback built into revision.
  • OperationsRun many translation efforts at once, with clear agency, timely and reliable data, and steady learning and adjusting.
  • ResourcingSecure varied, lasting support, with decisions and long-term stewardship moving increasingly to local and national leaders.
  • Talent pipelineKeep a steady pipeline of people ready for key roles in translation, access and engagement, and discipleship.

This takes a thriving environment, rooted in the convictions, aspirations, and commitment of national and local leaders and institutions. Within it, they grow in agency over the vision, quality, operations, and resourcing that keep translation, access, and engagement alive long after Seed Company’s involvement.
